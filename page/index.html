Reading the portfolio

Each account is compared with its own last year first. Campaigns peak in election years and committees between them, so raw growth rates across accounts aren't like for like.

Like for like leaves out accounts with no prior year. They count in totals but not in growth rates, projections against last year or new-donor change.

Raised per $1 covers texts and ads only, the costs Engine 9 tracks at every account. Rented lists, agency fees and staff time aren't in it.

Projections are pacing estimates, each account's Scorecard projection added up, not a forecast.

Definitions and how to read this page

Audience. Partners, owners and account leadership. This page shows every client side by side, so it must never be shared with a client. Message-level detail stays in each client's Fundraising Report.

Where each figure comes from. Raised and vs last year: All Donations. Projected year-end: the Scorecard projection, from last year's and 2022's pace, shown as a range. New donors: donors whose first gift in any record falls this year, against the same dates last year. Monthly run rate: the latest payment of each donor with a recurring gift in the last 35 days. Raised per $1: revenue from texts and paid ads over their tracked cost. Traced to a channel: revenue with a source code or message that maps to a channel.

Like for like. The headline total includes every loaded account. Growth, projection against last year, new-donor change, the monthly chart and the channel table leave out accounts with no full prior year, which show "New this cycle". A comparison year counts only if the account's gifts start by January 7 of that year.

Account type. Read from each client's account in Engine 9. An account with no type is left out of the portfolio and named in Reporting coverage.

Status. At risk: projected more than 15% behind last year. Watch: 5 to 15% behind. New this cycle: no full prior year. High-severity data-quality issues are not yet part of status.
