Reading the portfolio

  • Each account is compared with its own last year first. Campaigns peak in election years and committees between them, so raw growth rates across accounts aren’t like for like.
  • Like for like leaves out accounts with no prior year. Client K started this cycle, so it counts in totals but not in growth rates, projections against last year or new-donor change.
  • Raised per $1 covers texts and ads only, the costs engine9 tracks at every account. Rented lists, agency fees and staff time aren’t in it.
  • Projections are pacing estimates, each account’s Scorecard projection added up, not a forecast.
How to read this page

Audience. This page is for partners, owners and account leadership. It shows account-level results only; message-level detail stays in each client’s Fundraising Report.

Where each figure comes from. Raised and vs last year: All Donations. Projected year-end: the Scorecard projection. New donors: Acquisition. Monthly run rate: All Donations, recurring donors. Raised per $1: Scorecard, texts and ads. Traced to a channel and data-quality issues: Data Quality.

Period. The picker sets the period for every figure except the projection, which always covers the calendar year. Each compares with the same dates a year earlier.

Status. At risk: projected more than 15% behind last year. Watch: 5–15% behind, or one or more high-severity data-quality issues. New this cycle: no full prior year on record.
